AstraRTOS

CARM Cortex-M4STM32RTOS

What it is

A bare-metal real-time operating system for ARM Cortex-M4 microcontrollers. It handles task scheduling, synchronization primitives, timers, and low-level peripheral drivers — the layer between your code and the silicon.

What I've worked on

  • Reading through the scheduler and context-switch paths to understand how tasks actually get swapped.
  • Exploring synchronization primitives — semaphores, mutexes, and where each one falls over.
  • Working with timers and peripheral drivers on the STM32F4.
  • Implemented a simple MPU driver for memory protection.

What I learned

The gap between "I know what a context switch is" and "I can point at the exact instructions that make one happen" is enormous. Reading a real kernel closed some of that gap — and opened several new ones. You can check my notes and technical posts over in writing ↗.
